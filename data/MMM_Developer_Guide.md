## Executive Summary

The **Miami Makers Model (MMM)** transforms traditional real estate development by converting service providers into equity partners. Instead of paying contractors, architects, and consultants fixed fees, they become co-developers sharing both risk and reward.

### Key Results from NeVo Tower (North Bay Village):
- **$45M total cost** vs $53M conventional (15% savings)
- **34 months completion** vs 52 months conventional (35% faster) 
- **$7M cash required** vs $15M conventional (53% less capital)
- **28% IRR achieved** vs 18-22% conventional projection

## The Partnership Philosophy

### Core Principle
> **"Investors hope - partners make it happen"**

Traditional development relies on contractors who are paid regardless of project success. MMM aligns everyone's interests by giving service providers equity stakes, creating true partnerships where everyone wins or loses together.

### The Problem with Traditional Development
- **High Capital Requirements**: 25-30% down payments plus ongoing interest carry
- **Misaligned Incentives**: Vendors paid regardless of project outcome
- **Extended Timelines**: 48-60 months from concept to completion
- **Risk Concentration**: Developer bears all financial and market risk
- **Limited Expertise**: Reliance on hired consultants vs invested partners

### The MMM Solution
Partners contribute services at cost in exchange for:
1. **Proportional Ownership** based on contributed value
2. **Preferred Returns** of 8-12% annually before profit sharing
3. **Upside Participation** in project profits
4. **Tax Benefits** including depreciation advantages
5. **Long-term Value** through retained ownership

A: Partnership agreements include performance standards and buy-out provisions for non-performing partners.

A: MMM uses standard partnership structures with tax benefits. Consult qualified attorneys and CPAs for specific jurisdictions.

**Q: How do I convince partners to take equity vs cash?**
A: Focus on upside potential, long-term relationships, and shared success vs traditional payment risks.

**Q: Can I scale MMM across multiple projects?**
