## What is MMM?
**Miami Makers Model** converts service providers (contractors, architects, consultants) into equity partners instead of paying fixed fees. Everyone shares risk and reward.

## Proven Results (NeVo Tower)
```
COST:        $45M vs $53M traditional  (15% savings)
TIME:        34 vs 52 months           (35% faster)  
CAPITAL:     $7M vs $15M required      (53% less)
IRR:         28% vs 18-22% traditional (+10 points)
RISK:        60% reduction through shared accountability
```

## Core Benefits
- **Less Cash Required**: 50-70% reduction in upfront capital
- **Faster Delivery**: 35% timeline reduction through concurrent workflows
- **Lower Risk**: Distributed across invested partners vs single developer
- **Higher Returns**: Better IRR through capital efficiency
- **Quality Assurance**: Partners protect their investment
- **Network Effects**: Long-term relationships for multiple projects

## Partner Types Needed
1. **Construction Partners**: General contractors wanting equity vs fixed fees
2. **Professional Services**: Architects, engineers, consultants  
3. **Marketing Partners**: Sales teams with skin in the game
4. **Financial Partners**: Private lenders seeking higher yields

## Partnership Structure
```
CONTRIBUTIONS:
- Developer: Cash + management
- Partners: Services at cost + expertise

RETURNS:
- Preferred returns: 8-12% annually  
- Profit sharing: Pro-rata by ownership
- Tax benefits: Depreciation + partnership advantages

GOVERNANCE:
- Managing partner (usually developer)
- Major decisions by vote threshold
- Regular reporting and meetings
```
